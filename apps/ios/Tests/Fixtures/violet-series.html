Synopsis

A game developer suddenly finds herself trapped inside her own reverse harem game—as the villainous princess, Therese!

Just as she struggles to grasp the situation, a flamboyant man in a red suit appears.

“I’m Ozworld, your channel manager. Think of me as a broadcast PD—except our audience is the Constellations.”

A quest window pops up:
[Quest: Reach the true ending in hard mode]
Success: End the broadcast.
Failure: Death.

The only way to survive is to romance one of the four male leads. But Therese, the character she’s possessed, has the worst compatibility with all of them—and countless death flags!

Can she navigate the deadly romance routes, entertain the cosmic audience, and escape the game alive?
