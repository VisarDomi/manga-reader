Summary

Wrench, a scavenger boy, scrapes by with his father and brother, enduring endless bullying and oppression, yet he never wavers in his resolve to become a Mech Mechanic. But he doesn’t back down. Just like those senior Mech Mechanics who protected the mysterious Realm ten years ago, he takes control of the Spirit Mech without hesitation, battles his way from Scrap Iron Port into the invaders’ passage, the “Starfall Window,” and sets off on a fierce journey to protect his home and country!
