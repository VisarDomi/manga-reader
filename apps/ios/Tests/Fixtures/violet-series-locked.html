Synopsis

“I, Evelyn Felice, hereby resign as Empress. Gladly.”

An imperial court bound by rigid rules and a perfect yet cold husband, Fabian.
After turning thirty, Evelyn closed her eyes in solitude, only to wake up… and find herself twenty again.

Choosing freedom while protecting the most precious thing in her life, Evelyn thought she had severed her ties for good—but those old flames are beginning to burn once more.
