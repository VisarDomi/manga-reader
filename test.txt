Features:

1. Newer chapter loading
- On scrollend + 100 ms, each visible chapter may append its immediate newer chapter exactly once.
- Tracking happens exactly once per chapter

2. Position persistence
- On scrollend + 100 ms, save the current loaded image in the page URL.
- Providers own page URL recognition and construction.
- Opening or refreshing a saved URL restores the corresponding image.
