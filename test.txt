Definition:
- "may" means do it if its there

Reader:
- When scrolling settles, the chapter being read may append its immediate newer chapter exactly once.
- When scrolling settles, save the page at the screen's middle as the series' progress, and the exact point for a relaunch
- Opening a chapter at a saved page puts that page's top at the top of the screen; a relaunch returns to the exact point

Home:
- show all entries (the phone's copy at once, the site's in the background)
- the single local resume position for a provider and series always wins when present
- clicking on cover resumes
- clicking on chapters resumes the chapter (unread - start_page, read - end_page, partial - saved_page).

Special feature:
- swiping back from reader to home returns to the same home, instantly
- images that fail to load are asked again
- if resume/initial is unavailable, the reader says so (Chapter unavailable)
- every page has its real height before its image loads (from the site, or from the image on the phone)
